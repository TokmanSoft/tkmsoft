Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 18 March 2026 | Application: WordMind (com.tkmsoft.crosswordpuzzle)

This Privacy Policy explains how TKM Soft operates and protects your data while you play WordMind. We respect your privacy and design our application with a local-first, minimal data approach.

1. Age Requirement & Target Audience (18+)

WordMind is exclusively intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not target, market to, or knowingly collect any information from children or minors under the age of 18. If you are under 18 years of age, you are not permitted to use this application.

2. No Account Creation & Local Data Storage

WordMind does not operate a private backend server, user database, or member registration system:

  • All game progress, levels unlocked, card albums, and in-game coin balances are stored 100% locally on your device (using device-level SharedPreferences).
  • We do not have access to, nor do we store, your personal identity, real name, email address, or physical location on any external server.

3. Third-Party Services & Technical Telemetry

To ensure app stability, fix crashes, and display non-intrusive advertisements, WordMind integrates industry-standard Google SDKs. These third parties collect technical data subject to their respective privacy terms:

  • Google AdMob: Delivers rewarded and interstitial advertisements. (Google Ad Policies)
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Collects anonymous crash logs, app performance metrics, and device specifications (OS version, device model). (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play In-App Billing & Services: Securely handles in-app purchases and app integrity checks through Google's encrypted infrastructure. (Google Privacy Policy)

4. Advertising Identifiers & User Choices

Ad networks may use your Google Advertising ID (AAID) to limit ad repetition and provide relevant ad content. You have full control over this: you can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time via your device settings:

Android Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID

5. Data Deletion Rights

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations:

  • Local Game Data: Because all game progress is stored locally on your device, you can completely and permanently delete all your game data at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings > Apps > WordMind > Clear Storage or by uninstalling the application.
  • Telemetry Data: For any inquiries or requests regarding third-party diagnostic data, you may contact us at burkanemretokman@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

6. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

For players residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we respect your rights under the GDPR. We utilize a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) to obtain your explicit consent for ad personalization where legally required.

7. Security

Any interaction with third-party APIs (such as dictionary lookup queries and telemetry endpoints) is strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
