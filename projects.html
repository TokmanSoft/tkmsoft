MatFlow

The Artisan's Raw Material Cockpit

Engineered for the craftsperson who measures twice and creates once. Whether blending bespoke botanicals, baking sourdough at dawn, or formulating ceramics, MatFlow brings calculated tranquility to the chaotic dance between inventory and workshop creation.

  • Zero-Cloud Autonomy (Offline-First): Your secret formulas, unit costs, and inventory tallies live on your device with instant SQLite precision. No internet required, no trade secrets exposed.
  • Calculated Yields & Batch Deduction: Scale multi-step product recipes effortlessly. As you log completed batches, ingredients deduct with atomic mathematical certainty.
  • Critical Threshold Guardian: Gentle, timely visual cues alert you before essential raw materials run dry, keeping your workshop flow completely uninterrupted.
  • Sovereign Vault & CSV Export: Direct end-to-end cloud backup to your private Google Drive and instant CSV exports. No mandatory subscriptions, no predatory lock-in.
🌿 A quiet workshop ritual: opening the cockpit over a warm espresso to see exactly what can be created today.

PDF to Image

Uncompromising Privacy, Instant Local Extraction

Say goodbye to sketchy upload bars and privacy compromises. PDF to Image converts multi-page contracts, confidential financial audits, and high-res architectural blueprints directly on your device’s local processor with instantaneous tactile fluidity.

  • 100% On-Device Sovereignty: Zero cloud uploads. Your private documents, bank statements, and legal agreements never leave the physical perimeter of your phone.
  • Lossless Multi-Format Extraction: Render pristine PNGs, lightweight JPGs, or cutting-edge WebP images at up to 300 DPI Ultra HD native clarity.
  • Uninterrupted Background Flow: Powered by a resilient foreground engine, convert 200+ page documents seamlessly while multitasking without thermal throttling or memory crashes.
  • Instant Gallery & ZIP Archives: Batch export all pages straight to your Google Photos / Gallery album or compress into a clean ZIP archive with a single satisfying tap.
🔒 When handling confidential contracts or tax records, enjoy the absolute peace of mind that not a single byte touches a third-party server.
