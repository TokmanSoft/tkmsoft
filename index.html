WordMind

A Quiet Sanctuary for the Mind in a Noisy World

Put on your headphones and leave the rush of the world behind. The gentle glide of obsidian glass beneath your fingertips and the satisfying chime of every discovered word are crafted to offer a mindful, fifteen-minute escape in the middle of your day.

  • Zero Advertising Noise: No intrusive pop-up interruptions, synthetic timers, or predatory traps. Your flow of thought remains sacred.
  • Unbroken Calm: Commuting on the subway or up in the clouds, enjoy seamless play without an internet connection, always at your own tempo.
  • Frictionless Tactile Flow: An instantaneous touch response with zero latency—an understated sensory pleasure for your fingertips.
  • Night Obsidian & 24K Gold: A restful dark aesthetic that calms your eyes, accented by polished gold highlights whenever a puzzle unfolds.

☕ Best enjoyed with a fresh cup of coffee during your quiet hours of the day.

About TKM Soft & Our Philosophy

I am Burkan Emre Tokman. Under the banner of TKM Soft, far away from mass-production mobile factories, I develop independent games where every single pixel, haptic pulse, and typographic curve is treated with artisan care.

At the heart of every project lies a singular commitment: to honor your mind, attention, and time. No predatory monetization, no aggressive data collection. The vision is simple — to open a quiet, elegant, and genuinely enjoyable space in your everyday routine.
