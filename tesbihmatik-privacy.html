Privacy Policy for Tesbihmatik

Application: Tesbihmatik (Package: com.tkmsoft.tesbihmatik)
Developer: TKM Soft (Burkan Emre Tokman)
Contact: burkanemretokman@gmail.com
Effective Date: November 6, 2026
Target SDK: Android 15 (API 36) | 100% Ad-Free Prestige Project

Spiritual Sanctuary Promise: Tesbihmatik is maintained by TKM Soft as a non-profit, 100% ad-free, local-first spiritual sanctuary. There are zero banner ads, zero full-screen popups, zero rewarded videos, zero paywalls, and zero cloud accounts. Your worship records, daily zikr counts, and personal prayers never leave your device.

1. Local-First Architecture & Zero Data Collection

Tesbihmatik is designed with absolute privacy in mind:

  • On-Device Storage: All zikr tallies, target numbers, custom prayer lists, and streak statistics are stored locally on your device via encrypted Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences.
  • Zero Account Requirement: You never need to sign up, provide an email address, or create a password to use the application.
  • No External Server Transmission: The application does not transmit your worship logs, counting habits, or personal zikrs to any remote server or commercial cloud service.

2. Android Permissions & Technical Justifications

Tesbihmatik requests only the bare minimum hardware permissions required to deliver an authentic tactile and reminder experience:

Permission Type Purpose & Technical Scope
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal Enables the linear resonant actuator (LRA) haptic motor to simulate physical prayer beads and notify you with a distinct vibration when reaching target intervals (33, 99, 100).
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (API 33+) Delivers optional, user-configured prayer or daily dhikr reminder notifications. Users may disable notifications at any time in system settings.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Normal Allows the application to reschedule your custom daily reminder alarms if your phone restarts or powers down.

3. 100% Ad-Free Architecture

Unlike conventional mobile counters that monetize religious practices with intrusive advertisements, Tesbihmatik is permanently 100% ad-free:

  • No Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or third-party ad networks are bundled in the application.
  • No advertising identifiers (AD_ID) are accessed or shared.
  • All visual themes (Emerald, OLED Night, Royal Gold, Pearl White), acoustic sound sets, and widget extensions are completely free for all users.

4. Anonymous Technical Telemetry & Stability

To ensure high stability across thousands of Android device models and comply with Google Play Android Vitals standards (99.9%+ crash-free rate), the application includes Google Firebase Crashlytics:

  • Collects anonymized crash stack traces, device model, and OS version when an unexpected error occurs.
  • Diagnostic reports contain zero prayer names, personal counts, or identifiable user data.

5. User Data Sovereignty & Deletion

You retain absolute control over your records:

  • You can reset any active count or delete individual custom zikrs instantly inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing application storage in Android Settings permanently and irreversibly erases all saved counts and preferences from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Tesbihmatik is suitable for users of all ages and does not collect or solicit personal information from children or adults.

7. Policy Updates & Inquiries

If updates to Android OS specifications necessitate policy adjustments, changes will be posted on this official page.
